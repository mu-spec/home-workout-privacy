H Home Workout Privacy Policy How Home Workout handles your information. Effective date: November 18, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Home Workout handles information when you use the app. Home Workout is published by Muhammad Saad. The app is designed to provide workout guidance and help you manage your fitness activity on your device. 1. Information the app handles Home Workout does not require you to create an account. In the reviewed app version, the information described below is saved locally on your device so the app can provide its features: Fitness profile and preferences, such as information you enter to personalize workout recommendations. Fitness capability and progression information, including assessment results and progress used to adapt workouts. Workout history, which may include workout dates, exercise names and categories, sets, repetitions or work duration, rest periods, difficulty, and workout totals. Custom workout information, including workout names, selected exercises, workout structure, and target duration. Program progress, including the active program and completed activities. Reminder settings, such as the days and times you choose for workout notifications. App preferences, such as appearance and music settings. The app handles this information to operate its workout, personalization, history, custom-workout, program, reminder, and preference features. The exact information saved depends on which features you use and what you choose to enter. 2. How information is stored and used In the reviewed version, the information listed in this policy is stored in local app storage on your device. Home Workout does not operate an account system, app-owned cloud database, or developer-operated server for collecting or syncing this information. The app does not send this locally stored workout information to a Home Workout server. Because information is stored on your device, it may be removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The availability of device-level backups or transfers is controlled by your Android device and its settings. 3. Workout reminders and device information If you enable workout reminders, the app uses your selected schedule to create local notifications on your device. Android may require notification permission before notifications can be shown. The app uses the device's time-zone information to schedule reminders. The reviewed version does not request GPS location to create workout reminders. You can change or turn off reminders in the app or through your device's notification settings. 4. Voice coaching If you use voice coaching, the app passes workout or coaching text to the text-to-speech service available on your device. Depending on your Android device and its text-to-speech settings, speech processing may take place on the device or through a service provider chosen in your device settings. That provider's handling of information is governed by its own privacy terms. The reviewed version does not request microphone permission and does not record your voice. 5. Music and audio Workout music included with or selected within the app is played through your device. Your music-related preferences may be stored locally. The reviewed version does not operate a music account or upload your listening preferences to a Home Workout server. 6. Backup and restore If you choose to use the app's backup or restore feature, you can use the Android system file picker to save or select a backup file. A backup may contain the supported app data, including your fitness profile, capability profile, progression information, workout history, custom workouts, program progress, reminder preferences, and app preferences. The reviewed backup format is a JSON file and is not encrypted by Home Workout. Anyone who obtains an unprotected backup file may be able to read its contents. Store backup files somewhere safe and share them only with people or services you trust. If you choose a cloud storage provider through the Android file picker, that provider may store or sync the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Home Workout does not control that provider's handling of the file. 7. Sharing, advertising, and analytics In the reviewed app version, Home Workout does not include advertising, an advertising SDK, or an analytics SDK, and the developer does not sell your personal information. The app does not provide your locally stored workout information to data brokers or advertisers. Some device services that you choose to use, such as your device's text-to-speech provider or a cloud storage provider selected for a backup, operate independently and are subject to their own privacy policies. 8. Permissions The app may use Android notification and device scheduling capabilities to provide workout reminders, and file access through the Android system picker when you choose to back up or restore data. The reviewed release manifest did not declare permissions for location, camera, microphone, or contacts. App permissions and behavior may change in future versions; if they do, this policy will be updated as appropriate. 9. Data retention and deletion Information saved by Home Workout remains in the app's local storage until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android device behavior and any backups you have made. You can remove individual information where the app provides a relevant delete or reset option. Backup files are stored wherever you choose to save them. To delete a backup, use the file-management controls provided by the location where you saved it. Deleting the app does not automatically delete copies of backup files saved elsewhere. 10. Security Home Workout uses Android's app-specific storage for the locally saved information described in this policy. No method of electronic storage or file transfer is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any backup files you create, especially because the app's reviewed JSON backup format is not encrypted. 11. Children's privacy Home Workout is a general fitness app and is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account or online service. Because the reviewed version does not provide an account system or developer-operated data-collection server, the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through such a service. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of fitness apps and ensure that exercise activities are appropriate for the child. 12. Health and fitness information Home Workout provides general exercise guidance and fitness-tracking features. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Consider your health, abilities, and any advice from a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise program. Stop exercising and seek appropriate help if you experience pain, dizziness, or other concerning symptoms. 13. Changes to this Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be made available at the privacy-policy webpage linked from the app and its Google Play listing. The effective date at the top of the policy indicates when this version was prepared. 14.
